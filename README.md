# The Racing Drone

## About My Project

** What Is My Project? **
My Project is a Racing Drone built to tackle the high speed curves. With a very small body and a very intelligent mind this drone specifically has been designed for high speed racing but at the same time providing full video quality and percision.

** Why I Made This **
The Reason I made this goes back many years to when I was a young kid (around 6 years old) when I first aspired to make my own drone. I made designs some which later had motors here and there, clueless on how it would work just driven by the idea 'Drone'. Later when I started getting into Arduino which opened doors for me I understood how real drones would work but still unable to make my own. As this competition has come up it has given me the opportunity to fill my childhood dream and make my very own custom drone, built by my own hands and designed by my own hands.

## Key Features
** Key Features ** 
- High quality FPV Camera
- Custom Single Hand ESP Controller
- Motion Assisted Dynamic Tiltable Screen
- GPS Telemetry and Safety Functions

  ### Controller Pin layout
  Joystick:
  - X axis connected to pin 32 of ESP
  - Y axis connected to pin 33 of ESP
  - Joystick Button connected to pin 25 of ESP

 Rotary Encoder 1 (Screen tilt controller 1):
  - Rotation Counter connected to pin 26 of ESP
  - Direction Counter connected to pin 27 of ESP
  - Rotary Encoder button connected to pin 12 of ESP

 Rotary Encoder 2 (Screen tilt controller 2) :
  - Rotation Counter connected to pin 19 of ESP
  - Direction Counter connected to pin 23 of ESP
  - Rotary Encoder button connected to pin 14 of ESP 

 Rotary Encoder 3 (Camera tilt controller)
  - Rotation Counter Connected to pin 16 of ESP 
  - Direction Counter connected to pin 17 of ESP
  - Rotary Encoder button connected to pin 4 of ESP
